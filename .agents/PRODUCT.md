# OpenCoK Product Definition

## Scope

OpenCoK is an open-source, self-hosted Clash of Kings mod for independent communities, with its own server, rules, accounts and Flutter client targeting Android, iOS, Linux, Windows and Web. Elex is a visual/gameplay reference; its client, protocols, services and account/progress imports are unsupported. Platform targets require verification before release.

An installation can add kingdoms without a product-defined limit. Accounts are shared; characters and progress remain kingdom-specific. Organizers need approachable setup, administration and recovery; players need reliable access, fair progression and clear outcomes. Monetization through integrated payments/subscriptions is core: Console manages accounts/purchases, Admin manages kingdoms, Wiki explains supported content and Landing presents the project.

## Gameplay and progression

- Each character starts once at level 1 with a basic level-1 city, 100,000 wood, 100,000 food and 1,000 gold; stone, iron, silver and crystals start at zero. No completed upgrades or heroes are granted by faster progression. Returning to creation preserves existing progress.
- Hua Mulan is an infantry combat hero with three branching talent trees, target level cap 99 and one- or ten-level talents. Maximum percentage bonuses range from 50% to 500%; basic attributes/striking distance retain stated values. Recruitment/learning use normal fragments/points; portrait framing matches neighboring heroes. Other heroes display white rarity without a Legendary ribbon; Mulan retains hers. Visual changes preserve ownership, levels, talents and assignments.
- Construction, research, training, healing, fortifications, forging and workshop queues have explicit costs, deadlines and outcomes that survive interruptions without losing work or queue position.
- Production accumulates to a stated cap. Collection and rate/VIP/talent/equipment changes preserve earned resources and fractional progress.
- Quests, sign-in, activities and exchanges honor actual progress, reward periods, accepted costs, bonus order, level caps, alliance contributions and daily limits. Show offers/tokens/limits before spending; refresh exchanges once daily. Rewards remain claimable after failed confirmation; retries never duplicate rewards or levels.
- Placement/relocation requires verified kingdom rules and free space. Preserve older cities/progress; unresolved rules block placement without charging.
- Gathering parties may share resource points without attacking or exclusive ownership. Use owned troops/companions and march limits; preserve each party's earned share when others join/leave. Return when full or unavailable without duplicating troops, cargo or extra rewards. Preserve existing journeys, allowances and intentionally unavailable offers.
- Scouting preview is free and commits no army. Departure uses selected troops/companions and stated costs; reports respect visibility/protection and reach their owner. Explain unavailable targets; interruptions never invent combat outcomes, lose reports or duplicate charges.
- Spending rankings count confirmed expenditure, including large values, excluding previews, income and unverified history. Apply kingdom visibility, deterministic tie order and one-time final prizes, including mailed rewards.

## Automation

| Type | Expectations                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ---- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| NPC  | Organizer-controlled inhabitants that gather, attack and build; configurable location/count and pause/removal. Excluded from player payments, rewards, final rankings and statistics.                                                                                                                                     |
| Bot  | Paid routine automation on the buyer's accounts while offline, within manual rules. Cannot spend premium currency, change account settings, chat or give resources away. Player admission takes priority; players can pause/stop it.                                                                                      |
| Farm | Player-owned resource castle using normal production, combat and losses, with recorded loot/transfers. Bots may gather and automate owner raids; transfers follow published limits. Unless stated otherwise, exclude Farms from rankings, event rewards and alliance strength. Bound ownership and define who may attack. |

Plans disclose actions, limits and prices before purchase. Benefits require provider-confirmed payment and end on subscription expiry/refund; in-progress work stops or finishes safely. Organizers can inspect and pause automation per kingdom or globally without losing progress.

## Player and operator experience

Support static profile images and animated GIFs with explicit size, eligibility and replacement rules, normally once every three days. Successful replacement removes the old image and retains private confirmation without interrupting play.

Console shows purchases, subscriptions, Bots and Farms; failed redirects/confirmations never lose purchases. Admin actions identify the operator, reason and reviewable effects. Wiki describes only established behavior supported by the release.

Players distinguish pending/completed outcomes; organizers see health, unfinished work and slow actions. Optional outages explain unavailable features while preserving essential play. Protect personal data and ownership; support history explains resource changes, purchases and rewards without exposing private information. Maintenance preserves accounts, progress, kingdom history and personal images.

## Client and delivery

Deliver complete playable Flutter flows with approximately 70–80% qualitative fidelity to reference visuals/interactions, assessed through side-by-side scenes and recordings rather than historical feature counts. Preserve composition, scale/anchors, layers, camera movement, selection, contextual actions, transitions and animation. Use external assets only with applicable rights; do not depend on original binaries.

Begin with a representative city: composed scenery/buildings, animated water/ambient effects, pan/zoom, selection and server-backed construction. Validate desktop and narrow mobile viewports, reduced motion, frame timing, input, loading and resource budgets on released target platforms. Web previews alone do not prove native readiness.

Version client/server contracts and coordinate field/type/unit/identifier/configuration changes. Switching supported platforms/releases preserves identity, progress and economic outcomes. Updates need explicit migrations and recovery; original-service imports remain outside scope.

Priorities are the first city/construction flow, dependable core activities, interruption recovery, profile personalization, approachable kingdom management and paid automation with reliable entitlement/refunds. The first milestone enters a kingdom, loads a city, starts construction, leaves/returns and completes once with correct costs after server restart.

Success means organizers can open/restore kingdoms using available guidance, players complete responsive and recognizable flows without stuck work, and maintenance preserves progress. Paid automation delivers disclosed benefits within manual limits. Changes need an owner, affected releases, observable benefit and preservation plan; releases explain support/recovery, and unclear impacts require community input.

## Boundaries

OpenCoK is independent and not endorsed by Elex or other rights holders. MIT covers original code/documentation contributors may license, not third-party materials. OpenCoK distributes no proprietary Clash of Kings assets or trademarks; users must obtain external materials lawfully with required rights.

Support covers released OpenCoK clients and servers for independently managed communities, without promises of worldwide publisher-service parity or public hosting for unrelated communities.
